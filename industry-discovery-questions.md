# Industry-Specific Discovery Questions

Supplementary question banks for `/workflow architect` and `/function-chart architect`. Load this file when working with a specific industry to get targeted discovery questions beyond the universal set.

## Sales Process (Any Industry)

- How do leads come in? (inbound, outbound, referral, partner)
- What makes a lead "qualified" — what criteria do you use?
- Who handles the first contact?
- What happens during a sales meeting or consultation?
- How do you present pricing or proposals?
- What needs to happen for a deal to close?
- How do you hand off to delivery/operations?
- What's your follow-up cadence after a sale?

## Project-Based Businesses

- How do projects get kicked off after a sale?
- Who creates the project plan or schedule?
- How is work assigned to team members?
- How do you track progress against the plan?
- What happens when scope changes mid-project?
- How does a project officially close out?
- How do you capture lessons learned?

## Service Delivery

- How do customers request service?
- How is work scheduled and dispatched?
- Who performs the work?
- How do you ensure quality of the service delivered?
- What happens after the service is complete?
- How do you handle issues, callbacks, or rework?
- What's the feedback loop back to the customer?

## Recurring / Subscription Services

- How do clients onboard?
- What does the first 30/60/90 days look like?
- How do you measure client health?
- What triggers a renewal or expansion conversation?
- How do you handle churn risk?
- What's the escalation path when something goes wrong?

## Manufacturing / Production

- How do orders enter the system?
- What's the design-to-production handoff?
- How do you manage procurement and materials?
- What quality checks exist during production?
- How is finished product delivered?
- How do you handle defects or returns?

## Construction / Contracting

- How do you estimate or bid jobs?
- What happens between contract signing and start date?
- How do you coordinate subcontractors?
- What inspections or approvals are required?
- How do change orders work?
- What's the closeout process (punch list, final walkthrough, warranty)?

## SaaS / Technology

- How do users sign up or get provisioned?
- What does onboarding look like (self-serve vs. guided)?
- How do you handle support tickets?
- What's the feature request → development → release pipeline?
- How do you monitor system health and incidents?
- What's the billing and renewal process?

## Professional Services (Consulting, Agencies, Legal, Accounting)

- How do engagements start — what's the scoping process?
- How do you staff projects?
- What's the deliverable creation and review process?
- How do you manage client communication during an engagement?
- What does engagement closeout look like?
- How do you handle scope creep or change requests?

## Healthcare / Clinical

- How do patients enter the system (referral, self-schedule, walk-in)?
- What's the intake and assessment process?
- How is a care plan developed and communicated?
- What documentation is required at each stage?
- How do you coordinate between providers?
- What's the discharge or follow-up process?
- What compliance requirements affect the workflow?

## E-Commerce / Retail

- How do products get listed and priced?
- What's the order fulfillment process?
- How do you handle inventory management?
- What's the returns and exchanges process?
- How do you handle customer support?
- What marketing and promotional workflows exist?

## Financial Services

- How do clients onboard (KYC, compliance)?
- What's the account opening process?
- How are transactions processed and reconciled?
- What reporting obligations exist?
- How do you handle disputes or exceptions?
- What's the audit preparation process?

---

## Using These Questions

1. Identify the client's industry from the list above
2. Use the relevant question bank alongside the universal discovery questions in the architect skill
3. Adapt language to match the client's vocabulary — these are starting points, not scripts
4. If an industry isn't listed, ask the client: "What industry would you say is closest to how your business operates?" and use that bank as a starting point
